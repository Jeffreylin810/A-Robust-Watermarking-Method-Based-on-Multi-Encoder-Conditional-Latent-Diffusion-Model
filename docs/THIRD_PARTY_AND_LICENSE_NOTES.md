here.

## Third-Party Assets Not Included

The following categories are referenced for reproducibility but are not bundled
in this repository:

- large checkpoints
- generated image outputs

## User Responsibility

Anyone reusing this repository is responsible for:

- obtaining external datasets from their original or otherwise permitted sources
- checking the original licenses or terms of use for those resources
- ensuring their own use complies with those licenses and terms

Some files reference historical local paths and third-party project names
because they were part of the original experiment record. Those references are
included for provenance and reproducibility notes only. They do not imply that
the underlying assets are redistributed here.

internal branch names in summaries and comparison notes. Those references are
